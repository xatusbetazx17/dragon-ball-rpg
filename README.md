# Dragon Ball RPG

Pokemon-style turn-based fan RPG built with Godot 4 — walk the map,
battle by turns, level up, transform, and play the story from Kid Goku
through Dragon Ball Super, Moro and Granola. Non-commercial fan tribute.

Play: https://xatusbetazx17.github.io/dragon-ball-rpg/

Controls: WASD / arrows, or on-screen joystick and D-pad on phones.
Battle keys 1-7 or on-screen buttons.
